Who we are

About RoseCare Advocacy

RoseCare Advocacy is built on a team of passionate individuals with years of experience in community guidance, pastoral care, and counseling — and a deeply personal understanding of what it means to navigate the healthcare system during a crisis.

Florida is home to a large elderly population whose families often live out of state. We saw seniors feeling lost and overwhelmed — especially when health complications arise — and families feeling helpless from a distance. RoseCare exists to bridge that gap: serving as the caring, knowledgeable, boots-on-the-ground presence for those who need someone in their corner.

Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions

What does a patient advocate do?

A patient advocate works only for the patient and family — not for a hospital or insurance company. We attend appointments, coordinate between doctors, explain options in plain language, manage discharges, and resolve billing and insurance problems.

Do you work with families who live out of state?

Yes — it's one of the most common situations we serve. We act as your local, professional presence in Palm Beach County and keep you informed after every appointment. More on long-distance caregiving →

How much does a patient advocate cost?

Private advocates typically bill hourly, and every engagement starts with a free 20-minute consultation to scope what you need. See our full cost guide →

Is this the same as the hospital's patient advocate?

No. Hospital advocates are employed by the hospital. RoseCare is independent — we work solely for you, across every hospital, doctor, and insurer involved in your care.

Does Medicare cover a patient advocate?

Sometimes, in specific situations — and the rules changed recently. Read our Medicare coverage guide →
